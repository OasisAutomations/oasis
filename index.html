What you'll learn to build

Practical AI, aimed at the work you already do.

Not theory and not hype. Each topic starts with a real annoyance and ends with a tool that handles it.

AI agents that do one job well

Assistants that read, sort, draft, and remind, scoped to a single task you actually repeat.

Workflow automations

Spreadsheets, checklists, and email steps that run themselves, so you stop retyping the same things.

Small apps on your own device

Browser apps that install like a phone app, work offline, and keep data local. No servers to babysit.

Describing the job to AI

Explain a tool the way you'd explain it to a new hire, and report bugs like a punch list.

Testing and knowing when it's done

Sample data, honest limits, and the discipline to fix what bugs you and then stop.

Keeping data private

Local-first by default, so client and personal information stays where it belongs.

About Daniel

An estimator who learned to build with AI.

My background is construction takeoffs, bookkeeping, and office admin: Bluebeam, Excel, and a lot of repetitive work. I'm not a trained software developer. I started building my own tools with AI as a coding partner because I was tired of fighting the ones I had.

Now I build agents, automations, and small apps for my own work every week, and I document what works and what doesn't. Oasis Automations is where I teach that process to other people who'd rather build than wait for the perfect software.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this a business opportunity or MLM?

No. Oasis Automations is a paid learning community. There's no selling, no recruiting, no commissions, and no income promises. You join to learn to build tools for your own work.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. I didn't when I started. You'll learn to describe what you need to AI, test it, and fix it. Comfort with a computer and a spreadsheet is enough.

What's available today?

The blog, the YouTube build logs, and my public apps, all free. Templates, build-alongs, member Q&A, and the community space are planned for founding members and marked “Coming soon” until they're ready.

How much will it cost?

Pricing hasn't been set. Founding list members will hear first, and nobody is charged before membership opens.

What about my data?

The tools I build and teach are local-first where possible: your files stay on your device. In build-alongs we use sample data, never real client information.
